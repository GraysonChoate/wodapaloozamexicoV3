Be part of Wodapalooza Mexico City 2026

Wodapalooza Mexico City is coming in December 2026, and we want you to be part of the team that will bring the first edition in Mexico to life.

The volunteer program

Our Volunteer Program is designed for people who want to experience Wodapalooza from the inside, contribute their skills, and play an important role in delivering one of the world’s most exciting fitness events. We’re looking for volunteers with different backgrounds, skills, and levels of experience to support key areas before and throughout the event.

Volunteer areas

You can apply for one of the following roles:

  • Judges
  • Media & Content
  • Athlete Relations
  • Equipment
  • Athlete Recovery
  • Medical
  • Volunteer Relations

How to apply

Each area has different responsibilities and needs, so during the application process you’ll be able to tell us about your experience and select the role that best fits your profile. Our team will review all applications to find the best fit for each volunteer.

Apply to volunteer

More than helping us run the event, we want you to become part of the community, energy, and experience that will make Wodapalooza Mexico City 2026 possible.
